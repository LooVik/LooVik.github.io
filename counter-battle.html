Learning Udemy Unity

Counter Battle

Personal learning exercise — I followed a Udemy multiplayer FPS course and built the included project to learn. Not my own game design or a released product.

What is this?

This is a personal learning build only. I took Learn To Create An Online Multiplayer Game In Unity on Udemy (James Doyle) and worked through the course project — same structure, assets, and steps as the tutorial. I’m not selling this, not claiming the design or art as mine, and not presenting it as original portfolio work. It’s here to show what I learned about online multiplayer in Unity.

What I learned

  • How online multiplayer FPS projects are structured in Unity.
  • Photon — lobbies, rooms, and getting players into a match.
  • Networking basics — syncing players, health, and match state.
  • FPS gameplay patterns — movement, shooting, and match flow from the course.
