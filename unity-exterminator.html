Learning Udemy Unity

Unity Exterminator

Personal learning exercise — I followed a Udemy mobile game development course and built the included Exterminator project to learn. Not my own game design or a released product.

What is this?

This is a personal learning build only. I took Unity Mobile Game Development — Exterminator on Udemy (Jingtian Li) and worked through the course project — same structure, assets, and steps as the tutorial. I’m not selling this, not claiming the design or art as mine, and not presenting it as original portfolio work. It’s here to show what I learned about advanced Unity mobile development.

What I learned

  • Mobile project setup, touch controls, and camera follow for a third-person shooter.
  • Gameplay systems — inventory, health and damage, teams, projectiles, abilities, and shops.
  • AI perception and behavior trees for enemy logic.
  • Advanced C# patterns — interfaces, delegates, scriptable objects, and modular level workflow.
