Game Level Design

Personal Unity HDRP level — first-person exploration with CCTV screens, projection geometry, and trigger-based interactions.

What is this project?

A personal Unity level-design exercise built in HDRP. Walk through a 3D space laid out with planes, projection cubes, cones, and spot-lit zones. Security cameras feed live views onto in-world screens; trigger volumes let you swap CCTV angles or toggle a video display while exploring in first person.

What I built

  • Level layout — floors, projection primitives, lighting, and player spawn.
  • First-person movement and mouse-look camera.
  • Four CCTV feeds on render textures — switch active camera pairs with E and R at trigger zones.
  • Video screen toggle — show or hide a projection display when entering a zone.
