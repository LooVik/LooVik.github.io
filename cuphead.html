MugHead

Cuphead-inspired boss fight demake — team of six.

What is this project?

MugHead is a Cuphead-inspired boss-fight demake built by a team of six — Atari 2600-style visuals, multi-phase Floral Fury-style combat, three lives, and a boss that respawns tougher after each defeat.

What I contributed

  • Phase 3 boss fight — attack patterns, looping phases, and boss setup.
  • Boomerang attack — animation, prefab, and in-fight behaviour.
  • Boss flow and main scene integration for the final phase.
