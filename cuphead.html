Demake Action Game jam

MugHead

Cuphead-inspired boss fight demake with multi-phase combat.

An Atari 2600-style demake of the Floral Fury boss fight from Cuphead. The boss cycles through phases with new attacks as health drops. The player has three lives with no mid-fight pickups; defeating the boss awards score before it respawns tougher.

Designed and built with five teammates during a two-week game jam.
