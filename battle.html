Burglar Battle

Split-screen heist for PlayStation 5 — University of the West of England team project (~50 students).

What is this project?

Two teams of robbers race to loot the vault first — puzzles, challenges, and monsters in a split-screen heist built with Unity for PlayStation 5. Developed at the University of the West of England as a large team production (~50 students) in smaller groups.

What I contributed

  • Level design for the jail and underground areas.
  • VFX work across gameplay spaces.
