PS5 Unity Team project

Burglar Battle

A split-screen heist battle where two teams race to loot the vault first.

Two teams of two skilled robbers compete in a split-screen heist. Players solve puzzles, complete challenges, and evade monsters while racing to pull off the ultimate heist. Built for PlayStation 5 with Unity.

Developed with up to 50 students at the University of the West of England, organised into smaller production groups — a university console project with real pipeline constraints (not commercial employment work).
