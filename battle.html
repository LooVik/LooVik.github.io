Burglar Battle

Split-screen heist for PlayStation 5 — University of the West of England team project (~50 students).

Two teams of robbers race to loot the vault first — puzzles, challenges, and monsters in a split-screen heist built with Unity for PS5.

Developed at the University of the West of England in smaller groups within a large production. Not commercial employment work.
