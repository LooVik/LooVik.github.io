War of Tank

Mobile team tank battler — pick a map and a mode, then fight AI tanks 5v5 or 3v3 alongside bot teammates.

What is this project?

War of Tank is a personal Unity project built for mobile. Players choose a map and a mode — 5v5 or 3v3 — then drop into a team battle against AI tanks, with bot teammates fighting alongside them.

Matches play out in low-poly desert arenas built around hangars, radar towers and rock formations that double as cover. Teams are colour-coded so you can read the fight at a glance on a small screen, and a compact HUD tracks surviving allies and enemies, your kill count, and your remaining health.

What I contributed

  • Chibi tank model, enemy tank prefab, and the spawn object that places both teams at match start.
  • Map and mode selection — 5v5 or 3v3 — feeding team sizes into match setup.
  • AI tank behaviour: movement, turning, and repeating shots with physics-driven projectiles.
  • Bot teammates that fight alongside the player on the same side.
  • Hit detection and damage reporting into a central game manager that tracks the match state.
  • HUD showing surviving allies and enemies, kill count, and remaining health.
  • Low-poly desert arena laid out around hangars, radar towers and rock cover, built to read clearly on a phone screen.
