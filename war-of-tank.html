War of Tank

Ongoing personal Unity project — tank prefabs and combat scripts are in place; the main scene is next.

What is this project?

War of Tank is a personal Unity project I’m picking back up — originally started around location-aware / AR-style play with Mapbox in the stack. I already built a chibi tank model, enemy prefab, paint-ball and water-ball ammo, and movement/shooting logic, but the main game scene was never finished. That’s the part I’m working on next.

What I contributed

  • Chibi tank model, enemy tank prefab, and tank-factory spawn object.
  • Tank behaviour — timed movement, random turns, and repeating paint-ball shots with physics.
  • Paint ammo with random colour assignment on spawn.
  • Water-ball hit detection wired toward a central game manager.
  • Mapbox / AR Foundation groundwork in the project — main scene and game loop still to come.
