Play House Mobile App

Unified iOS/Android app combining Entrance, T1, T3, and T7 from the Play House educational platform.

Role: Project Manager / Senior Unity Developer

Period: 2025–2026

Platform: iOS and Android

App experiences: Entrance, T1, T3, and T7

Technology: Unity, C#, Firebase SDK, Addressables, Spine, Tilemaps, mobile safe areas, REST APIs, localization, rhythm-game systems

What is this project?

The Play House Mobile App is the native iOS and Android adaptation of the Play House educational platform. Where the WebGL version runs as separate browser-based theatre experiences, the mobile app merges Entrance, T1, T3, and T7 into a single unified application. Users authenticate once, navigate between educational experiences inside one app, and retain shared player data and session continuity across theatres — with every interface adapted for touch, safe areas, and varying phone and tablet screen sizes.
