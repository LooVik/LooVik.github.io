About

Game Developer · Malaysia

I'm a Game Technology graduate from the University of the West of England, passionate about creating games that challenge and inspire players. I build with C# in Unity and Unreal. I spent roughly three years in commercial Unity development at Star Splash (Itchi Technology), where collaboration and clear feedback mattered as much as clean code. I'm looking for new opportunities in the games industry and hope to keep learning from experienced mentors. Above all, I want to make games that are fun, innovative, and accessible to everyone.
