LockedIn

Short PSX-style horror — 4-week university jam.

What is this project?

LockedIn is a short PSX-style horror game made during a four-week university jam — low-poly retro visuals, tight spaces, and environmental tension.

What I contributed

  • Player movement and core controls.
  • PSX-style look — low-res render treatment, retro materials, and scene mood.
  • Sound design — footsteps, weather, breathing, and ambient audio.
  • Level layout plus door, locker, menu, and win/end screen animation.
