FlappyAnimals

A Flappy Bird-inspired portrait arcade game built in C++20 with SFML 3.

Platform: Windows prototype

Tools: C++20, SFML 3, CMake, doctest

Status: In development — Android and iOS are planned.

What is this project?

FlappyAnimals is my ongoing C++ arcade project. Guide a bird through an endless course of pipes, earn a point for each gate cleared, and try to beat your best score for the session. As the score rises, the course becomes faster and the gaps tighten.

Gameplay and technical highlights

  • Flap movement, gravity, pipe generation, and collision detection.
  • A fixed 60 Hz simulation with render interpolation for smooth movement.
  • Ready, playing, death, and results states, with replay, session-best scoring, and score-based medals.
  • Animated pixel-art birds, velocity-based tilt, and flap, score, and collision sound effects.
  • Gameplay logic separated from SFML rendering and audio, with doctest unit tests that run without a game window.

Controls

Press Space or click to start and flap. On the results screen, press Space or click to reset for another run. Press Escape to close the game.
