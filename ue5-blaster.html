Learning Udemy Unreal Engine 5

UE5 Blaster

Personal learning exercise — I followed a Udemy UE5 multiplayer shooter course and built the included Blaster project to learn. Not my own game design or a released product.

What is this?

This is a personal learning build only. I took Unreal Engine 5 C++ Multiplayer Shooter on Udemy (Stephen Ulibarri) and worked through the course project — same structure, assets, and steps as the tutorial. I’m not selling this, not claiming the design or art as mine, and not presenting it as original portfolio work. It’s here to show what I learned about competitive multiplayer in Unreal Engine 5.

What I learned

  • UE5 multiplayer architecture — Game Mode, Game State, Player State, and replication basics.
  • Online Subsystem sessions, matchmaking, and a reusable multiplayer plugin pattern.
  • Lag compensation — client-side prediction and server-side rewind for fast-paced shooters.
  • Weapons, pickups, HUD, match states, and server-authoritative gameplay flow.
