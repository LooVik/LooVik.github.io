What is this project?

Fun with Chinese 2 (友趣學中文2) is an educational mobile game developed as part of the C-for-Chinese@JC programme, funded by the Hong Kong Jockey Club Charities Trust. Designed for non-Chinese speaking children aged 3 to 8, the app uses a role-playing game structure — children explore islands, complete quests, and earn rewards while learning Mandarin through play.

The app features immersive audio storybooks covering themes such as self and identity, family, school, community, and intercultural awareness, plus nine types of interactive mini-games with progressive difficulty to build listening, speaking, and reading skills. The live app includes multiple islands with dozens of storybooks and accompanying games.
